    Emissions cartogram

    Each circle is a country, sized by its CO₂ and placed as close to its real location as the circles allow. Show a single year, or every year from the first to the last added together.

    • Emissions are annual CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry, excluding land-use change, as recorded in your file. 1 Gt (billion tonnes) = 1,000 Mt.
    • Colour scales are logarithmic and fixed across years, so a shade means the same amount in every year. Hatched countries have no figure for that year.
    • Maps use present-day borders, so earlier years are drawn on today's outlines. Antarctica is left off.
    • Share of world is each country's percentage of the world total in your file, a total that also counts international aviation and shipping. Bar colours come from a country-to-continent lookup whose totals match the file's continent rows.
    • Continent totals are the file's own continent rows. They leave out international aviation and shipping, which the file doesn't assign to any continent.
